# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As members and contributors of the Predicting Mortality in Heart Failure Patients Classifier project, we are committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, collaborative, and welcoming environment for everyone involved in the project.

We value diversity and recognize that every team member brings unique experiences, perspectives, skills, and ideas. We are committed to ensuring that participation in this project is free from discrimination, harassment, intimidation, and any form of disrespect, regardless of a person's gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, race, ethnicity, age, religion, nationality, educational background, or level of technical experience.

We believe that a supportive environment encourages learning, innovation, and meaningful collaboration.

## Expected Behaviour

All project members and contributors are expected to:

- Treat everyone with respect, kindness, professionalism, and consideration.
- Welcome different opinions, experiences, and perspectives.
- Communicate constructively and provide respectful feedback during discussions, code reviews, and project meetings.
- Listen actively and give everyone an equal opportunity to contribute.
- Respect team members' time, responsibilities, contributions, and boundaries.
- Collaborate transparently and take responsibility for assigned tasks.
- Acknowledge the contributions and ideas of others.
- Maintain confidentiality and respect the privacy of team members and individuals whose information may be encountered during the project.
- Use inclusive language in all project communications. For example, use "Hello everyone" instead of expressions that may exclude members of the team.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are considered unacceptable:

- Discrimination or harassment based on personal characteristics or identity.
- Bullying, intimidation, personal attacks, or threatening behaviour.
- Insulting, offensive, derogatory, or deliberately disrespectful comments.
- Unwelcome sexual language, jokes, or imagery.
- Public or private harassment, including repeated unwanted contact.
- Sharing another person's private or confidential information without their permission.
- Deliberately excluding, undermining, or disrespecting another team member.
- Disrupting collaborative activities or intentionally preventing others from contributing.
- Any other conduct that creates an unsafe, hostile, or unwelcoming environment.

## Consequences of Unacceptable Behaviour

The project team is committed to maintaining a respectful and collaborative working environment. When a team member or contributor engages in behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, the team will take appropriate steps to address the situation.

Depending on the nature and severity of the behaviour, the following actions may be taken:

1. **Informal discussion:** The team may discuss the concern with the individual involved and clarify the expected standards of behaviour.

2. **Formal warning:** If the behaviour continues or is more serious, the individual may receive a formal warning from the team.

3. **Instructor involvement:** Where the behaviour is serious, repeated, or beyond the team's ability or authority to address, the matter will be brought to the DSCI 522 course instructor for guidance and appropriate intervention.

## Reporting and Handling Incidents

Any team member or contributor who experiences or witnesses behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct is encouraged to raise the concern with the project team.

Concerns may be reported privately by emailing the project team at **lindaart@student.ubc.ca**. Reports will be treated confidentially and reviewed by the team.

The team will make reasonable efforts to address reported concerns through respectful communication and discussion. The confidentiality of individuals involved will be respected to the extent possible.

If an incident is serious, involves repeated misconduct, cannot be resolved within the team, or falls beyond the team's control or authority, the matter will be escalated to the DSCI 522 course instructor for guidance and appropriate action.

The project team commits to:

- Listening to concerns respectfully and without judgment.
- Reviewing reported incidents fairly and objectively.
- Making reasonable efforts to resolve disagreements and concerns within the team.
- Seeking the course instructor's guidance when necessary.
- Respecting the privacy and confidentiality of everyone involved.

No individual should face retaliation or discrimination for reporting a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related activities, including GitHub repositories, pull requests, issues, code reviews, meetings, online discussions, and other spaces where individuals represent or participate in the Predicting Mortality in Heart Failure Patients Classifier project.

All project members and contributors are expected to uphold these principles throughout the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently.

By adopting this Code of Conduct, all project members commit to maintaining a collaborative environment where everyone feels respected, valued, and able to contribute meaningfully.
